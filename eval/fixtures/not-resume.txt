Nasi Lemak Recipe

Ingredients:
2 cups jasmine rice, 1 cup coconut milk, 1 cup water, 2 pandan leaves knotted, 1 tsp salt.
For the sambal: 15 dried chillies soaked, 5 shallots, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp tamarind paste, 2 tbsp sugar.

Method:
1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Cook with coconut milk, water, pandan and salt.
2. Blend the chillies, shallots and garlic into a paste. Fry in oil on low heat for 20 minutes until the oil separates.
3. Add tamarind and sugar, simmer 5 more minutes.
4. Serve the rice with sambal, fried anchovies, peanuts, cucumber and a boiled egg.
